How this estimate works

Uses unit costs, population, health, resistance, attack damage, attack timing, range, and movement speed.

Raw power ranks those combat measures only. General-purpose and Robust gently reward frontline, ranged, and mobile role coverage; they do not guess the opponent.

Explore mode leaves stock uncapped. Available-stock mode keeps food, wood, stone, and metal separate and reports the tightest stock share.

It does not yet model counters, armour interaction, technologies, formations, terrain, production time, or micro.
